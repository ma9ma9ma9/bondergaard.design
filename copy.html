Overview

In a FieldAI interview, a construction crew member said he’d heard that moving materials takes up 15–20% of the hours on a typical job. He imagined asking a cart to take his gear up a floor. How do you hand a robot a job and know it understood, is making progress, and has finished?

I designed Copy as a push-to-talk interface for a shared robotic cart. A worker gives the cart a job by voice, checks the pickup and drop-off it heard, and gets back to work. Their phone shows which cart is coming, where it’s headed, and when to expect it. When a route is blocked, the cart finds another way and sends the worker a revised delivery time.
